# Social Media Do's and Don'ts

## ✅ Do’s
- Think before posting content online
- Use privacy settings to protect personal information
- Share only verified and accurate information
- Communicate respectfully with others
- Maintain a positive and professional online presence

## ❌ Don’ts
- Do not share sensitive personal information publicly
- Avoid spreading fake news or rumors
- Do not engage in online arguments or trolling
- Avoid posting inappropriate or offensive content
- Do not click on suspicious links or messages
