# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

## General Safety Tips
- Never share OTPs, passwords, or PINs with anyone
- Always check website URLs before entering personal information
- Avoid clicking on suspicious links in emails or messages
- Use strong and unique passwords for different accounts
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible
- Keep your apps and devices updated regularly

## UPI / Financial Safety Tips
- Always verify the receiver’s UPI ID before sending money
- Do not approve collect requests from unknown people
- Never scan unknown QR codes to receive money
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions
